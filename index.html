What is honest here 🧾

Speaking advice travels with famous numbers, and several of the most famous do not say what they are quoted as saying. Each claim on these pages carries its status.

ClaimStatus
Every bench figure: pace, sentence length, pause marks, fillers, the break buttonReally computed in your browser from derived counts for 2,299 one-speaker TED talks (Kaggle, rounakbanik/ted-talks); no transcript text is shipped
Whether faster speech is understood lessNot measured by the bench, and said so on the widget; one 1970 study of machine-compressed speech is read at source in session 4, with its limits
"Words are 7% of the message" (Mehrabian 7-38-55)Misquoted scope. Mehrabian's own page limits it to feelings and attitudes; session 2 shows what that means
"Say 'I am excited' before you speak"Read at source, with its one direct replication, which did not reproduce the observer ratings, taught alongside it in session 3
"People fear public speaking more than death"Traced in session 3 to a 1973 survey reported in a London newspaper, whose original report could not be read; a 2024 survey puts the two fears within three points of each other
The 10/20/30 rule of slidesAn opinion, from its author's 2005 blog post, with no study behind it
